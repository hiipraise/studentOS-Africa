# Monetisation policy

StudentOS Africa earns trust by being useful before it is commercial. Any
revenue stream must preserve that trust.

## Allowed

- Clearly labelled sponsorship of a newsletter issue or a page section.
- Affiliate links to tools and services that are already editorially accepted.
- Paid job or programme listings from verified employers, labelled as such.
- Grants or donations that do not buy editorial influence.

## Not allowed

- Paying to be marked verified, featured or “recommended”.
- Undisclosed affiliate links or sponsored copy written to look editorial.
- Predatory products: high-interest loans, unaccredited degree mills, paid
  “guaranteed” scholarships, visa agents, or anything that asks students to pay
  to apply for a free opportunity.
- Selling or sharing student emails, report data or analytics.
- Sponsored content that contradicts the content SOP (unverified deadlines,
  invented eligibility, copied aggregator text).
- Ads that disguise themselves as official provider pages or application forms.

## Labelling rules

1. Sponsored placements carry a visible “Sponsored” label in the card and the
   detail page, before the call to action.
2. Affiliate links carry a short disclosure near the link and are logged through
   `/go/[slug]` like every other outbound click.
3. Newsletter sponsors are named in the issue, with the same label a web reader
   would see.
4. A sponsorship never changes the score, ranking or eligibility verdict in the
   Finder.

## Editorial independence

- The editor decides what is listed and how it is described.
- A sponsor can see the final copy before publication and may withdraw, but
  cannot require a claim the team cannot verify.
- Corrections are published even when they affect a sponsor.
- Community reports and availability evidence are never edited to favour a
  commercial partner.

## Data and measurement

- Sponsored campaigns receive aggregate counts only: impressions, outbound
  clicks, saves. No individual student data is shared.
- Tracking stays first-party and is covered by the privacy notes.
- No third-party ad pixels are added without updating the privacy inventory.

## Approval

Any new revenue stream needs a written decision from the owner covering:
who pays, what is labelled, where it appears, how long it runs, and how it can
be removed. Log the decision in `docs/IMPROVEMENT_LOG.md`.
